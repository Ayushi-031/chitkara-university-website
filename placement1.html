About Us

The Placement Cell at chitkara university is dedicated to providing students with the necessary tools, guidance, and opportunities to kickstart their professional careers. Our mission is to bridge the gap between education and industry by offering personalized career counseling, skill development workshops, and access to top-tier companies through campus recruitment drives. We work closely with leading organizations across various sectors to ensure that our students are exposed to a wide range of career opportunities. From resume building and mock interviews to internships and job placements, our Placement Cell ensures that every student is well-prepared to succeed in the competitive job market. Additionally, we foster valuable connections with alumni and industry professionals, providing students with a strong network for future career growth and development. At chitkara university, we are committed to shaping the leaders of tomorrow by offering comprehensive support throughout their career journey.
