About Chitkara University

Chitkara University, established in 2002, is a leading private university in Punjab, India. It offers diverse undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across multiple disciplines including engineering, business, health sciences, and more.

Our Programs

Programs in various branches such as Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and more.

Master of Business Administration (MBA)

Specializations in Marketing, Finance, Human Resource Management, and more.

Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

A comprehensive program for those aspiring to specialize in computer applications.

Campus Life at Chitkara

Our vibrant campus is equipped with modern facilities to enhance learning and student life. With hostels, sports facilities, and numerous clubs and activities, there is something for everyone at Chitkara University.
